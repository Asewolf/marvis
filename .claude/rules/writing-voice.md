# Writing voice

Applies to anything going out under your name. Posts, email, client copy, documentation,
replies. Using an assistant to write is fine. Sounding like one is the problem.

Replace this with your own voice. What follows is the shape, and a starting list.

## Banned phrases

The giveaways. Grep for them before anything ships.

- "It's not X, it's Y"
- "Let's be honest" / "Here's the truth" / "Here's the kicker"
- "In today's world" / "In an era of"
- "Most people get this wrong"
- "Let that sink in"
- "Cut through the noise"
- "Game changer"
- delve, leverage, unlock, seamless, robust, crucial, landscape, harness, deep dive
- "It's worth noting", "That said", "At the end of the day"

## Banned structures

These are the tells that survive a find-and-replace.

- **The rule of three** when there are only two real points
- **Chopped fragments for rhythm.** "Fast. Simple. Done."
- **Rhetorical questions that go nowhere**
- **Saying the same thing three ways** in consecutive sentences
- **Self-congratulating closers.** "And that's what matters"
- **Summary closers.** "In short", "Bottom line", "To summarise"

## Punctuation

- **No em dashes.** The single loudest tell. Use a comma, a full stop, or restructure
- Same for en dashes and double hyphens used as dashes
- Normal hyphens in compounds are fine

## How to apply

Run this as a pass BEFORE anything ships, not as a rewrite afterward. Grep the draft for
the lists above. One hit is one line to rewrite, not a reason to start over.

Add to it. Every time you edit the same phrase out by hand, that phrase belongs on the
list.
